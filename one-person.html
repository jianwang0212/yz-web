Berklee graduation work / 伯克利毕业作品

一个人 做不好

写给 Berklee 同学和老师的一首歌。我要毕业回国了，所以想写一首关于离开的歌——但不是伤感告别，而是一封有 6/8 律动、有铜管、有笑声的感谢信。 一个人做不好，还好你们都在。 A song for my Berklee friends and teachers before I graduate and return to China—not a sad farewell, but a grooving thank-you letter in 6/8, carried by horns, rhythm, and the people who stayed. No one does it alone. I am grateful you were all here.

为什么写 / Why this song

我明明大你们一轮，却总是你们撑住我。

排练室、舞台、抢不到的录音房，都记得我们一起熬到天亮。我要离开 Berklee、回到中国时，真正舍不得的不是一栋楼，而是那些一起写旋律、改编排、迟到、录音，又一次次重新来过的人。 The rehearsal rooms, stages, and impossible-to-book studios remember the nights we carried into morning. As I leave Berklee and return to China, what I will miss is not a building, but the people who wrote, rearranged, arrived late, recorded, and began again with me.

所以它必须是欢快的。离开不是把这段路变成过去，而是承认：我能走到现在，本来就不是一个人完成的。 That is why the song had to feel joyful. Leaving does not turn the journey into the past; it simply admits that I never arrived here alone.

一个人做不好 还好你们都在 No one does it alone.

创作手记 / Production notebook

告别可以很欢快，和声也可以不走直线。

The song began as a compact brief: fewer words, stronger repetition, a physical 6/8 groove, and harmony with enough tension to feel alive.

01 / References

听感坐标

《男孩》· 梁博《爱在》· 方大同Uptown Funk · Bruno MarsDeadman · 蔡徐坤《太坦白》· 李荣浩
02 / Pulse

6/8 + groove

参考《爱在》的 6/8 推进和《太坦白》的 groove，让离开听起来像继续往前走。

03 / Color

Rhodes + horns

Fender Rhodes 承担和声底色，sax 与 trumpet 用 line 回应人声，而不是只做铺垫。

04 / Reharm

Constant structure

用 constant structure、diminished、tritone substitution 与 minor 7 flat 5，让重复段落保持移动。

05 / Tension

♭9 · 13 · ♯11 · ♭13

Dominant 和弦加入 altered tensions，旋律也允许停在 13、♭9 和 ♭13 上，不急着解决。

朋友的名字 / Names in the room

把你们的名字留在歌里。

创作时为朋友设计的 call-and-response：让乐器和玻璃后面的人不只留在 Credits，也直接进入歌的记忆。 / The original roll-call idea gives the people behind the instruments and the glass a place inside the song's memory.
